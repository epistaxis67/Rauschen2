Petra Ritter

talk imminent
Charité / Berlin Institute of Health · Johanna Quandt Professor of Brain Simulation, Director of the Brain Simulation Section · co-founder of The Virtual Brain · coordinates eBRAIN-Health (digital twins) and TEF-Health · ERC Consolidator Grant · trained physician, doctorate under Villringer
Role ① find the signal — also touches ③ (review instance via TEF-Health) and ④
Why her
She works at both edges of the noise concept at once, without ever resolving the tension. In the foundational line (with Freyer, Breakspear) noise is structured, necessary, individually measurable and meaning-bearing. In the clinical-applied line (Virtual Brain, Alzheimer's) it is a constant, an artefact — or switched off entirely. No one else on the contact list embodies this contradiction so completely.
Six notions
At least six distinguishable senses run through her papers: the removed (gradient artefact, ballistocardiogram, physiological noise) · the null hypothesis (filtered Gaussian noise as a model of nothing happening) · the ingredient (necessary to model a living system) · the placeholder (the name for everything the model omits) · the appearance (desynchronised real signals that look like noise) · the measurand (individual parameters, variance, heterogeneity).
Three anchors
  • BuzsákiHe reasoned that very large events in 1/f systems ought to be inevitable, then observed they never occur. In the paper by Freyer, Ritter and Breakspear (2009) the response is that the problem was not observing them but forming an adequate null distribution for their absence. Whether the case may be read that way is precisely our open question — see the manifesto.
  • Sigma = 0.01In the Alzheimer's study with 124 participants (Zimmermann et al. 2018) coupling, excitation and inhibition are optimised individually. Only the noise parameter stays fixed at the same value for everyone — although a 2012 paper showed it can be estimated per person.
  • The zero lineIn her own Alzheimer's work (Stefanovski et al. 2019, she last author) the integration is deliberately deterministic, "in the absence of noise". The result is a silent state that she herself calls unphysiological.
Her own line
In the discussion of a 2018 eLife paper it says, in substance, that methods for physiological noise correction might remove variance from fMRI experiments that relates to neuronal activity — and thereby exclude relevant information. That is our thesis, in her own publication.
Questions
1
You have written that physiological noise correction might remove neurally relevant variance. Has anything changed in your field's practice since?Her own published insight, set against the routine.
2
Buzsáki held large events to be inevitable and found they never occur. The paper shows they were there — the null distribution was missing. How often is that the real reason one doesn't see something?Our guiding thesis, on a case from her own work. Asked openly, not as a claim.
3
You fitted everything individually for 124 people — only the noise stayed at the same value for all of them. Why?The documented gap. A "we never looked at that" would be a find.
4
In your measurements you remove noise; in your simulations you add it so the model becomes realistic. Is that the same noise?The core tension, addressed directly.
5
At weak coupling you describe the network input as noisy fluctuations — although it is all real regional signals. Is noise sometimes just signal without synchrony?Serres' included third, in her language. Without naming him.
Caution
On the Freyer papers she is co-author, not first author (corresponding: Breakspear) — do not present that phrasing as her personal position. On Stefanovski 2019 she is last author; on the 2012 paper she is among those who conceived the study. And: her work also shows hard technical limits (locked raw signals, sampling rates too low, 230,000 compute hours). Our thesis only holds in the more modest cut — sometimes the limit is not the technology.
Practical
Her open dataset (50 people, 22 min simultaneous EEG-fMRI, ages 18–80) contains raw and processed data plus the ECG channels — one could take the difference, i.e. what the correction removed. Access runs via a data processing agreement sent directly to her; she decides personally. A question one can ask her.

It is the working layer between the Contacts (who is worth approaching) and the Questions (how to run the conversation). The general question set still applies to everyone; what stands here is what bites only with this one person — because it comes from their own papers.

The value lies not in confronting experts with their own sentences, but in starting where they have themselves marked something as open. Where someone published an insight and their field's routine did not follow it, there is a question no one else is asking.
