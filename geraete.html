What Measures Us

Thought from the practical level: which devices collect data about humans today, how accessible they are, what they capture — and where in the filtered-out material the greatest potential lies to turn former noise into signal. Three steps, then the link back to the mindmap.

Step 1

The devices — by accessibility

Sorted from "almost everyone has one" to "specialist lab." Price and spread determine how easily large, everyday data volumes can be gathered — the precondition for searching the noise for structure at all.

Ubiquitous · already present≈ everyone · €0 extra
  • Smartphone — camera, mic, IMU, GPS, touch, typing dynamics
  • Smartwatch / fitness band — Apple, Garmin, Fitbit, Samsung
  • Headphones / earbuds — motion, sometimes ear-PPG
Consumer wellness · affordable≈ €50–400 + subscriptions
  • Smart ring — Oura, Samsung; PPG, temp, motion
  • Recovery band — Whoop (subscription)
  • Smart scale / BP cuff — Withings et al.
  • OTC CGM — glucose, over the counter (Libre, Dexcom Stelo)
  • Consumer EEG — Muse, Emotiv; forehead/ear electrodes
  • Smart home — sleep mat, camera, voice assistant
Prosumer / semipro · elevated≈ €500–5,000
  • Multichannel EEG headset — dry, mobile
  • fNIRS headband — cerebral blood flow
  • Chest strap / medical patch — ECG, EMG, breathing
  • mmWave / radar sensor — contactless vitals
  • Eye tracker — gaze, pupil, microsaccades
Clinical / research · lab≫ €10,000 · setup needed
  • Clinical EEG / MEG · OPM-MEG
  • fMRI / PET
  • Polysomnography — sleep lab
  • Lab omics — blood, methylation, microbiome
Step 2

What they capture — useful signal & side channel

Left: what the device sells as a product. Right: the side and "noise" part — raw and interfering signals usually filtered, averaged or discarded internally, and precisely therefore the unrecovered material.

DevicePrimary useful signalSide / "noise" channel (usually discarded)
Smartwatch / ringHeart rate, HRV, steps, sleep stages, SpO₂, skin temperatureRaw PPG waveform (pulse shape, not just the beat), raw 3-axis acceleration, micro-tremor, signal-quality "dropouts"
SmartphoneLocation, steps, app usage, call/message cadenceTyping & swipe dynamics, tremor via camera/IMU, voice-mic variation, nightly movement patterns
Mic / voiceVoice commands, dictation, callsProsody, jitter, shimmer, pause structure — paralinguistic features irrelevant to transcription
Consumer EEG / fNIRS"Focus"/"calm" scores, neurofeedback, meditation metricsAperiodic 1/f part, artifacts from pulse/breathing/motion, transition states between wake and sleep
CGM (glucose)Glucose trend curve, alarms, time-in-rangeShort-term variability & curve shape around meals/stress, sensor noise texture, nightly micro-fluctuations
Radar / cameraPresence, breathing/heart rate, fall detectionFine movement, restlessness, gait micro-variation, rPPG skin-color shift, ambient "clutter"
Eye trackerGaze point, dwell time, saccadesMicrosaccades, pupil micro-oscillation, blink rhythm — classically treated as measurement noise
The decisive bottleneck: no major manufacturer releases the continuous raw signals (PPG, acceleration, temperature) — almost only the processed end values and occasionally short ECG snippets are exportable. Which means: the "noise" side channel that holds the unrecovered signal is already discarded inside the device. Whoever reaches it (own capture, open hardware, research APIs) has access to material closed off to most — and that is itself part of the opportunity.

Step 3

Where the greatest potential lies

New setups, ordered by potential to turn former noise into signal. Criterion: (a) the raw material is accessible or reachable, (b) it is currently discarded, (c) there is a plausible real quantity behind it.

01

Raw-signal fusion across many everyday devices

Mindmap branch 03 · 05 · 08 — aggregation + neural/physiological noise
Idea
Instead of the processed scores, merge the raw side channels of many cheap devices of one person (PPG waveform + acceleration + typing dynamics + voice + optionally CGM) and couple them over time.
Why strong
Combines two mindmap levers: aggregation of noisy sources (√N principle) and side channels that count as noise individually. The raw material is reachable given enough will; almost no one evaluates it combined.
Possible signal
State/load index, early deviation from personal norm, brain–body–behavior coupling.
Caution
Data access (raw signals locked) and spurious correlations with many channels × few people. Without the review instance (QUEST, branch 04), pseudo-signals arise easily.
02

The aperiodic EEG / fNIRS part as the target

Mindmap branch 08 · 09 — neuro-measurement + longevity
Idea
Don't reduce consumer EEG/fNIRS to the "focus score," but deliberately capture the 1/f floor — what the devices compute away as baseline noise — and track it longitudinally.
Why strong
The most mature case in the whole mindmap: the former noise is already documented as an age/state marker. Hardware is affordable, at-home measurement repeatable.
Possible signal
Repeatable brain "state"/aging readout; an effect measure for interventions.
Caution
Consumer EEG is artifact-prone; the clinically documented effects are small. The "rejuvenation" reading goes beyond the evidence (see contact Antonenko/Fleischmann).
03

Paralinguistics from the microphone

Mindmap branch 08 — extended nerve/state measurement
Idea
Not what is said but the how — jitter, shimmer, prosody, pauses — read from voice data that accrues anyway, as a state/health signal.
Why strong
Microphones are everywhere; the paralinguistic trace is thrown away for transcription. Very low hardware threshold.
Possible signal
Markers for fatigue, mood, airways, sometimes neurological change.
Caution
Strongly privacy-sensitive (voice = identifying, content = intimate). Consent and local processing are not optional here but a condition.
04

Contactless fine movement (radar / rPPG)

Mindmap branch 07 · 08 — environmental sensing + periphery
Idea
From what radar/camera sensors discard as ambient "clutter" and fine restlessness, read out gait micro-variation, nightly restlessness, or skin-color pulse (rPPG).
Why strong
Fully contactless, everyday-capable, sensors getting cheaper. The discarded part is especially large here.
Possible signal
Early indicators (gait, restlessness) for neurological or cardiac change; sleep fine structure.
Caution
Passive capture without the person's active involvement raises consent questions; maturity and validation still low.
05

CGM fine structure beyond the trend

Mindmap branch 02 · 08 — physiological fluctuation
Idea
Not the glucose trend curve, but the short-term variability and curve shape — the "texture" around meals, stress and sleep — as a signal in its own right.
Why strong
OTC CGM is newly affordable and already popular in the tech/longevity scene; the fine structure is barely used so far.
Possible signal
Metabolic flexibility, stress reactivity, individual food response.
Caution
Sensor noise and trend easily confused; causality hard. Risk of over-reading everyday fluctuation.

// link to the mindmap — and the recurring pattern

All five setups are variants of one move: take what a device discards as by-product, artifact or baseline noise, and ask whether a real quantity hides in it. Exactly the red-marked spots of the mindmap (branches 02, 05, 08, 09) meet here on the practical level of devices.

Two conditions decide success, and both are already in the mindmap: access to the raw material (the bottleneck from step 2 — the raw signals are locked) and the test against pseudo-signals (branch 04). The real scarcity is not the noise — there is plenty everywhere — but access to the unfiltered signal and the honest test of whether it is real.
